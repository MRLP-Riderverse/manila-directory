# Horseboyy

## Public notes
Horseboyy, also operating as HORSIN AROUND, is a Manila-based selector working across house, breaks, techno, and booty / club. Documented through Manila Community Radio, he belongs to the post-pandemic wave of local DJs rebuilding the city’s intimate underground circuit.
