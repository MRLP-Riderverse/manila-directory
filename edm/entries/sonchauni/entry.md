# Sonchauni

## Public notes
Sonchauni is the project of Assi Abogado, a Filipino-born artist based in Dubai and active across both the UAE and Manila underground. Their work connects DJing, visual direction, and collective building, with a focus on textured, high-intensity sets rooted in queer club culture.
