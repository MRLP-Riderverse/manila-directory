# Sonchauni

## Public notes
Sonchauni works between Dubai and Manila, balancing ambient euphoria with percussive tension. Using Dubai as a base and Manila as home, her transit-shaped practice reflects a diasporic artist workflow sustaining the city’s wider underground network.
