## Description
Tucked behind its iconic red door in the heart of Poblacion, Apotheka remains one of Manila's most consistent underground institutions. The main floor leans into baile-funk, global club, and house; the mezzanine delivers sharper Detroit techno, jungle, and '90s electro.
